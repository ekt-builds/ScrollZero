Sideloading Walkthrough

Install & Unlock in 3 Quick Steps

Because ScrollZero uses Android's AccessibilityService and is distributed directly via APK outside the Play Store, Android's security layers require two one-time confirmations.

1

Download & Bypass Google Play Protect

Required for APKs

Independent APKs that declare Accessibility permissions automatically trigger a Google Play Protect warning because the signing certificate isn't on Google Play.

Option A — Quickest

When the Play Protect popup appears after tapping app-release.apk, tap More details → Install anyway.

Option B — If "Install anyway" is hidden
  1. Open Google Play Store → tap your Profile icon → Play Protect → Gear icon.
  2. Temporarily toggle OFF Scan apps with Play Protect.
  3. Install app-release.apk, then turn Play Protect back ON.
Power user tip (Skips Play Protect & Restricted Settings): adb install app-release.apk
2

Unlock "Controlled by Restricted Setting"

Android 13+

Android greys out Accessibility for sideloaded apps until you grant restricted settings once:

  1. 1. Tap ScrollZero in Accessibility first: Open ScrollZero → Open Accessibility Settings → tap ScrollZero (so Android registers the request).
  2. 2. Open App Info: Go to Settings → Apps → ScrollZero (or tap Open ScrollZero App Info inside the app).
  3. 3. Allow restricted settings: Tap the ⋮ (More) menu in the top-right or bottom corner (or Other permissions on HyperOS) → tap Allow restricted settings.
  4. 4. Toggle Accessibility ON: Return to Downloaded apps / Accessibility and switch ScrollZero ON.
3
